# Funding from the European Regional Development Fund found to drive employment and innovation

*By Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Action | BMWE*

The Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy (

) published the final report of a commissioned study entitled

“Evaluation of the ERDF programmes in the funding period 2014–2020 in Germany.”

The study shows that German regions benefit from the European Regional Development Fund (ERDF) in numerous ways. Proven effects of the funding include a significant increase in employment and innovation among supported companies, reductions in greenhouse gas emissions and the development of new public research infrastructures with international appeal. In Eastern Germany in particular, the funding is associated with a clear positive contribution to economic performance. In Western Germany, less economically developed regions have reaped a larger share of the benefits.

is currently preparing intensively for the upcoming negotiations on

Cohesion Policy after 2028. In light of limited financial resources, it is more important than ever to demonstrate the effectiveness of European funding. This study on the European Regional Development Fund (ERDF) allows the German government to showcase the tangible effect

support has had on employment, innovation, climate action, and regional development in Germany. These investments in companies and public institutions are visible signs of European solidarity. The study combines comprehensive company-level data with innovative methods, providing a level of depth not yet seen in ERDF evaluation. This provides a solid basis for making the

structural funds future-proof and effective.
