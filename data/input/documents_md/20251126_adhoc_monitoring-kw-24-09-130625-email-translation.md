Monitoring Week 24 (09.-13.06.25)

• Federal Council Resolution "Protecting Consumers in Online Shopping - Quick Measures in Connection with Offers from Third Countries on Online Platforms" / Motion by the State of Baden-Württemberg

The resolution is to be presented at the 1055th session of the Federal Council on June 13, 2025, and subsequently assigned to the Legal Affairs Committee as lead committee, with the Committee on European Union Affairs, Finance Committee, and Economic Affairs Committee as advisory committees.

• 1st Reading of the AfD Bill to Abolish the Act on Corporate Due Diligence Obligations to Prevent Human Rights Violations in Supply Chains (Supply Chain Due Diligence Abolition Act – LkSAG)

The AfD parliamentary group is calling in a motion for the swift abolition of the EU Supply Chain Directive (CSDDD, Directive (EU) 2024/1760). To this end, the Federal Government should "use all means at its disposal at EU level to work towards the quickest possible suspension and abolition of the CSDDD". [...] The representatives argue that the CSDDD creates bureaucratic requirements that go far beyond the German Supply Chain Due Diligence Act (LkSG) to be abolished. [...]

Dr. Markus Reichel (CDU) sharply criticized the AfD's motion to abolish the Supply Chain Act and accused it of a national and anti-European stance. While the Union also recognizes the bureaucratic burden of the law and advocates for a revision in terms of practicable European regulations, it rejects complete elimination without replacement. Reichel particularly emphasized the distortion of competition by platforms such as Temu and Shein, which are currently not subject to the same due diligence obligations as German companies. This is a grievance that needs to be remedied within the framework of a new, European-coordinated regulation.

• Press Release from the Federal Cartel Office: Federal Cartel Office has concerns about the application of so-called price control mechanisms by Amazon

The Federal Cartel Office has sent Amazon a preliminary legal assessment in which it evaluates the price specifications for merchants on the Amazon Marketplace as potential abuse of market power. Specifically, this concerns Amazon's practice of regulating merchant prices through algorithmic price controls and setting certain price limits, the exceeding of which can lead to restrictions in visibility or even removal of offers. According to the authority's view, Amazon is thereby impermissibly interfering with the merchants' freedom to set prices. The rules are said to be non-transparent, arbitrarily set, and could lead to merchants being unable to cover their costs – with the consequence that they are pushed out of the marketplace. The authority sees this as a possible violation of Section 19a(2) GWB, Section 19 GWB, and Article 102 TFEU. Particularly problematic is that Amazon acts as both platform operator and competitor on the Marketplace and could distort competition through the price limits. The proceedings are based on extensive investigations, including a merchant survey, and are being conducted in close coordination with the EU Commission and the Federal Network Agency. Amazon can now comment on the allegations.

• BNetzA as DSC certifies three additional institutions as Trusted Flaggers

The following organizations have submitted an application for approval and meet the criteria for approval:

o Federal Association of Online Trade (BVOH) with a focus on commercial legal protection and unfair competition, particularly on online marketplaces; o HateAid gGmbH with a focus on digital violence, fraud and deception, particularly on social media platforms o Federation of German Consumer Organizations (vzbv) with a focus on online marketplaces and social media platforms on the topics of consumer rights, product safety, online trade, fraud.

These organizations were certified because they have demonstrated special expertise and competence in detecting, identifying and reporting illegal content according to Article 22(2) DSA. In addition, they are independent of online platforms and ensure careful, accurate and objective transmission of reports to online platforms.
