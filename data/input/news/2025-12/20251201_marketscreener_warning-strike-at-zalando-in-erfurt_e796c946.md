# Warning Strike at Zalando in Erfurt

ERFURT (dpa-AFX) - The Verdi union has once again called for a warning strike at online fashion retailer Zalando, deliberately choosing the high-sales pre-Christmas period. "While Zalando entices customers with discounts, celebrates record revenues, and aims to keep the logistics center in Erfurt running at full speed, employees are making one thing clear: Without good working conditions, there will be no smooth shipping," the union stated.

Union Accuses Company of Ignoring Employees

According to union secretary Matthias Adorf, 50 colleagues participated in the night shift and 60 to 70 in the early shift during the warning strike. "It is completely incomprehensible that Zalando ignores the interests of its employees," Adorf emphasized. Revenues are "gigantic"--especially now during the so-called Black Week and Cyber Week.

Zalando Points to 14.5 Percent Increase in Pay

The company was initially unavailable for comment. In October, during a previous warning strike in Erfurt, Zalando pointed out that salaries at the Erfurt site have risen by a total of 14.5 percent since 2024, and vacation entitlement has been increased to up to 30 days. Employees also receive additional benefits such as social counseling, leave for volunteer work, or free shares. At that time, the company stated that the warning strike did not cause any restrictions for customers.

Collective Agreement Would Mean Wage Increase of Over 10 Percent

Verdi is demanding recognition of the collective agreement for retail and mail order workers in Saxony, Saxony-Anhalt, and Thuringia. According to the union, this would mean more than a 10 percent wage increase for Zalando warehouse workers, as well as higher bonuses, special payments, shorter weekly working hours, a collectively agreed company pension plan, and other improvements.

According to Adorf, this would not be a problem for Zalando. However, the company is trying to avoid a collective agreement at all costs. "But that's exactly what brings security: fair wages, reliable bonuses, and a say in working conditions. Zalando wants to prevent that." A central strike assembly for all shifts has been scheduled for Tuesday.

## [Company Profile](https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/ZALANDO-SE-18100088/company/)

Zalando SE is the European No. 1 in the online distribution of shoes and clothes for women, men and children. The group's activity is organized around 2 areas :
\- online retail of clothing, footwear and accessories. The group also operates the 'Lounge by Zalando' private sales site;
\- sale of solutions and services to businesses: integration of partner brands within the Zalando platform, sale of multi-channel fulfilment solutions, logistics services, development of advertising solutions, sale of solutions enabling physical shops to sell their products online via the Zalando platform.
At the end of 2024, the group had 51.8 million active customers.
