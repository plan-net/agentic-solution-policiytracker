# Zalando Announces Share Buyback Program to Fulfill Stock-Based Compensation

BERLIN (dpa-AFX) - Online retailer Zalando has announced a new share buyback program aimed at meeting obligations under its stock-based compensation plans. The company will repurchase up to 5.5 million of its own shares at a total purchase price (excluding transaction costs) of up to 100 million euros, Zalando reported Wednesday evening after the close of trading in Berlin. This initiative is intended to fulfill commitments arising from stock-based compensation programs for employees and executive board members. The buyback program is set to begin on November 6, 2025, and will end no later than December 31, 2025. The shares will be repurchased via the stock exchange, the company added. In after-hours trading, Zalando shares rose by more than 2 percent compared to the Xetra closing price.

On Thursday, the DAX-listed company is scheduled to present its quarterly financial results.

## [Company Profile](https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/ZALANDO-SE-18100088/company/)

Zalando SE is the European No. 1 in the online distribution of shoes and clothes for women, men and children. The group's activity is organized around 2 areas :
\- online retail of clothing, footwear and accessories. The group also operates the 'Lounge by Zalando' private sales site;
\- sale of solutions and services to businesses: integration of partner brands within the Zalando platform, sale of multi-channel fulfilment solutions, logistics services, development of advertising solutions, sale of solutions enabling physical shops to sell their products online via the Zalando platform.
At the end of 2024, the group had 51.8 million active customers.
