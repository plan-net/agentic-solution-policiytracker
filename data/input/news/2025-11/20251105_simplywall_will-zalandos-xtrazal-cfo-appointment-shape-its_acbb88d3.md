## Zalando Investment Narrative Recap

To invest in Zalando, shareholders need to believe that the company can maintain growth and improve margins despite challenges such as ongoing margin pressure and slow consumer demand in Europe. The appointment of Anna Dimitrova as CFO, effective 2026, adds expertise in financial integration but does not materially alter the key short-term catalyst, realizing cost synergies from the ABOUT YOU acquisition, or reduce the most significant risk, which remains margin pressure driven by investments and high return rates.

One relevant recent announcement is the July 2025 completion of the ABOUT YOU acquisition, which is now included in Zalando's latest guidance for 2025 revenue and GMV. This acquisition is integral to current margin and profit improvement expectations, with successful integration playing a critical role in whether these synergies are realized as forecast.
