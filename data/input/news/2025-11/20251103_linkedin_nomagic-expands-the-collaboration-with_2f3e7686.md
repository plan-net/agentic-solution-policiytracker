🚨 BREAKING:
[Nomagic](https://pl.linkedin.com/company/nomagic?trk=public_post-text) expands the collaboration with [Zalando](https://de.linkedin.com/company/zalando?trk=public_post-text)! 👟
That's huge news for European robotics scene, as it shows we can create great technologies, then scale them and make the biggest companies use them.
Nomagic has been selected by Zalando to scale robotic automation across its European fulfilment centres. 🇪🇺
The collaboration builds on a successful pilot where Nomagic’s AI-powered “Richard” robots handled item-level picking, scanning, and induction, achieving an industry-leading 10,000 picks per day.
Following those results, Zalando will expand to nine robots live in 2025 and a double-digit fleet in 2026! 📈
Nomagic’s systems combine computer vision and machine learning to manage the massive variety of fashion and lifestyle products in Zalando’s warehouses (if you want to know what's the diversity, just check their website and see how many different product they store, and then sell 🤯).
For Zalando, the technology supports employees by automating repetitive tasks, improving reliability, and maintaining customer service standards across an increasingly complex logistics network.
[Kacper Nowicki](https://pl.linkedin.com/in/kacpernowicki?trk=public_post-text), [Tristan d'Orgeval](https://de.linkedin.com/in/tristando?trk=public_post-text), keep rocking guys! 🦾💚
