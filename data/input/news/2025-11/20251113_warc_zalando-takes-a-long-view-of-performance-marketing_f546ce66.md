Zalando takes a long view of performance marketing 
 Clothing &amp; fashion retail 
 Brand management 
 Strategy 
 
 Online fashion retailer Zalando has bumped up its investment in performance marketing and is prepared to wait up to a year and a half to recoup the investment of acquiring new customers via this channel. 
 What’s happening   
 
 Zalando has stepped up its marketing spend during 2024, from 7.4% of sales last year to 9% today.  
 During Q3, performance marketing spend increased to take advantage of a strong start to the autumn/winter season, with a less promotional environment and more full price sales.  
 The payback period for new customers acquired through performance marketing channels stands at 450 days, CFO Sandra Dembeck told an earnings call.  
 Zalando is also investing in brand marketing campaigns such as “What Do I Wear”, which provides fashion inspiration and guidance. 
 Associated content formats, such as the Zalando Fashion Assistant (which now has one million users), are increasing engagement. “There are early indications that this is really improving the time spent on the platform as well as delivering growth,” Dembeck reported. 
 Zalando Partner Marketing Services, which includes retail media, is growing double digit; the goal is for this division to reach 3% to 4% of B2C GMV within the next four years. 
 
 Why it matters 
 Performance marketing is often seen as having a short-term focus, driving revenue for the next quarter. But Zalando reminds us that there is more than one business metric to consider.
