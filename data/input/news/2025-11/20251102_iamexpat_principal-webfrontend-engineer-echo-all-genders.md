# Principal Web/Frontend Engineer - Echo (all genders)

Posted on November 1, 2025

Posted on November 1, 2025

[Apply for this position](https://www.iamexpat.de/auth/login)

The Zalando eCommerce Platform Sciences team assists customers in their fashion discovery journey and helps them find what they are looking for across Zalando. In the echo department we drive the vision of having Zalando be the place for customers to discover quality content, find personalised inspiration and make a truly deeper connection. We are transforming the content discovery systems, utilising innovative approaches and building inspirational experiences for our customers.

As a Principal Engineer you will work with exceptional engineers, science, analysts, and product leaders to spearhead the strategy, design, and implementation of ML & AI backed experiences that are scalable, repeatable, and secure. We are looking for a strong technical leader to help build experiences that allow customers to explore and discover fashion in a unique style. You will partner with your Product counterparts to define and deliver new ways to discover fashion and lifestyle across all Zalando experiences.

Teams at our Berlin Tech Hub work on many of the cornerstones of our Fashion Store, including personalization and recommendation components. Through full stack engineering and research, we turn visionary ideas into new emerging businesses with real potential. By tackling diverse challenges for the fashion industry, we drive the personal digital experience and underlying knowledge models to create the most inspirational fashion experience for our customers.

## WHERE YOUR EXPERTISE IS NEEDED

- Guide the engineering department that helps customers discover quality content, find personalised inspiration and have deeper engagement with Zalando.
- Scale up the teams building innovative solutions leveraging latest in machine learning technologies including generative AI and large language models.
- Support and lead teams in achieving a high level of engineering quality, throughput, performance and reliability.
- Drive operational excellence as part of your team culture.
- Mentor seniors to principal engineers and actively collaborate with other teams. Share knowledge and expertise within our talented engineering, product, data and science community.
- Develop and implement strategies and approaches to solving complex problems and challenges to help achieve business goals and objectives.
- Work closely with applied science and engineering leaders, product managers and other business stakeholders to bring state‑of‑the‑art solutions to customers and identify new opportunities.

## WE’D LOVE TO MEET YOU IF

- Growth mindset, strive for excellence, fast and passionate learner, open‑minded, excel on giving and receiving feedback, thrive in front of changes and challenges.
- Excellent software development engineering skills to design and implement effective solutions for data and ML strategies in large‑scale production environments. You are great at and happy to hands‑on delivering projects as well as leading development.
- Experience leading the architecture and design (design patterns, reliability and scaling) and building of new and current data systems, and developing tools for self‑serve access.
- You have hands‑on experience with React Native, JavaScript/TypeScript, React Hooks and state‑management libraries like Redux, MobX, Context API. A familiarity with native modules and bridging for iOS and Android is a plus.
- Experience with GraphQL, JSON, networking libraries, WebViews and software design patterns and paradigms. Understanding and experience with MVC, MVVM, Flux and other architectural patterns.
- You write clean, maintainable code with tests, have a deep understanding of observability and take ownership of your work.
- You make the applications resilient and reliable through open tracing, monitoring, and load testing.
- Experience working with machine‑learning backed customer experiences is a plus.
- Good communication skills and experience leading organization‑wide collaborations. Ability and eagerness to understand the business context where the team operates and the customer problems being solved.
- Experience coaching and mentoring senior engineers, and working closely with applied scientists, engineers, and product managers.
