## [Company Profile](https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/ZALANDO-SE-18100088/company/)

Zalando SE is the European No. 1 in the online distribution of shoes and clothes for women, men and children. The group's activity is organized around 2 areas :
\- online retail of clothing, footwear and accessories. The group also operates the 'Lounge by Zalando' private sales site;
\- sale of solutions and services to businesses: integration of partner brands within the Zalando platform, sale of multi-channel fulfilment solutions, logistics services, development of advertising solutions, sale of solutions enabling physical shops to sell their products online via the Zalando platform.
At the end of 2024, the group had 51.8 million active customers.
