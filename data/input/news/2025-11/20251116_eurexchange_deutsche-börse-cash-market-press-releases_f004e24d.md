### [Deutsche Börse Photography Foundation introduces new Curatorial Fellowship](https://eurexchange.com/dbcm-en/newsroom/press-releases/Deutsche-B-rse-Photography-Foundation-introduces-new-Curatorial-Fellowship-4742584)

- The Foundation launches an open call for a 10.000 Euros Curatorial Fellowship (2026-2028) for mid-career curators worldwide.
- The new initiative aims to support curatorial practice, foster dialogue on contemporary photography, and expand the collection’s diversity.
- Curators are invited to develop an exhibition based on works of the Foundation’s collection and propose new acquisitions.
- The international call for applications is now open until 31 December 2025.

Press Release \| Deutsche Börse Photography Foundation

### [This November in Paris: The Deutsche Börse Photography Foundation exhibits and promotes artists and the academic dialogue](https://eurexchange.com/dbcm-en/newsroom/press-releases/This-November-in-Paris-The-Deutsche-B-rse-Photography-Foundation-exhibits-and-promotes-artists-and-the-academic-dialogue-4742556)

- During the international photography fair “Paris Photo,” the Foundation will present an extensive programme reflecting its dedication to **collecting, exhibiting and promoting** contemporary photography.
- The exhibition “Face to Face” shows selected **works from its collection of contemporary photography at “Paris Photo”** in the Grand Palais.
- In another exhibition, the Photography Foundation, in collaboration with **the Goethe-Institut Paris**, will focus on young photographers and show works by students from the University of Applied Sciences and Arts, Hanover.
- Finally, the two **prizes for research and journalism in the field of photography** will be awarded jointly with the German Photographic Society (Deutsche Gesellschaft für Photographie, DGPh).
