## **Marigold Annouces Loyalty Essentials**

Marigold announces Loyalty Essentials, empowering QSR and Retail marketers with the path to launch a loyalty program within as little as 30 days.

For marketers in need of establishing a powerful, scalable, data-driven loyalty program, Marigold’s Loyalty Essentials provides an intuitive, self-service platform to drive repeat business, personalize customer engagement, and build lasting customer loyalty. And all without the cost or complexity of a traditional enterprise solution.

Marigold believes loyalty program features should be available in an approachable, scalable package that can provide marketers the opportunity to maintain and update member profiles and data to better personalize communication with centralized customer insights. ## **Revolutionizing Retail: xTool Retail Studio Presents Full In-Store Customisation Solutions at ShopTalk Europe 2025**

At **Shoptalk Europe 2025** (2–4 June, Fira Gran Via, Barcelona, Booth K33), **xTool** will unveil its all-in-one in-store customization solution, empowering retailers to drive customer engagement and increase revenue through **real-time, on-demand personalization**. Visitors will experience live demonstrations of the **xTool F1 Ultra** and its laser engraving system, offering a **plug-and-play solution** that requires minimal training.

### Transforming Retail: The Power of Customisation

xTool Retail Studio combines **laser engraving** and **apparel printing** in a versatile, **turnkey solution** designed for modern retailers. The system includes:

- **Laser Personalisation:** Customers can personalize jewelry, keychains, electronics, sports gear, and more with the **xTool Retail Marker** (Class 1 certified). A self-service kiosk and easy-to-use interface allow customers to engrave names, messages, or custom designs.
- **Apparel Customisation:** In-store printing with **G7-standard color** allows customers to design and personalize a range of products, from t-shirts and hats to denim jackets, all completed in minutes.

These solutions go beyond selling products—they create memorable **experiences** that drive **foot traffic** and **boost sales**.

### Real Impact in Retail

“Retail is no longer just about products; it's about creating moments,” says **Jasen Wang**, CEO of xTool. “Brands like Stanley, Timpson, and Lacoste have used xTool to transform stores into hubs of experience. Stanley’s global pop-ups, for example, saw a 40% increase in foot traffic after launching live engraving with xTool.”

### Key Benefits for Retailers:

- **Drive Organic Engagement**
- **Unlock Consumer Insights**
- **Increase Revenue Per Order**
- **Reduce Return Rates**
- **Differentiate from E-Commerce**

### Trusted by Leading Global Brands

xTool is already helping brands enhance in-store experiences, including:

- **Prada Beauty**: Luxury engraving at flagship stores.
- **Decathlon**: On-site gear personalisation at local events.
- **Clarks Originals**: Customisation of removable leather patches for the iconic Wallabee shoe.
- Other major brands include **L'Occitane**, **Ralph Lauren**, **Lacoste**, and **Woodcraft**.

### Flexible, Tailored Solutions for Every Retailer

The **xTool Retail Studio** includes **laser engravers**, **apparel printers**, self-service kiosks, and **real-time design previews**. Customisation options extend from UI design and templates to colour schemes and logos, ensuring seamless integration with your brand’s identity.

### Global Reach, Localised Support

With teams across the U.S., Europe, Japan, Thailand, and China, xTool provides **installation**, **onboarding**, and **extended warranties**. The system is supported by fast repairs, guaranteed response times, and localisation of software and materials for global consistency.
