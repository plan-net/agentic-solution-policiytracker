# ZIM Integrated Shipping Services (ZIM) Q4 Earnings and Revenues Top Estimates

March 12, 2025 — 08:15 am EDT

Written by
Zacks Equity Research for
[Zacks->](https://www.nasdaq.com/publishers/zacks)

ZIM Integrated Shipping Services (ZIM) came out with quarterly earnings of $4.66 per share, beating the Zacks Consensus Estimate of $3.47 per share. This compares to loss of $1.23 per share a year ago. These figures are adjusted for non-recurring items.

This quarterly report represents an earnings surprise of 34.29%. A quarter ago, it was expected that this container shipping company would post earnings of $7.45 per share when it actually produced earnings of $9.34, delivering a surprise of 25.37%.

Over the last four quarters, the company has surpassed consensus EPS estimates three times.

ZIM, which belongs to the Zacks Transportation - Shipping industry, posted revenues of $2.17 billion for the quarter ended December 2024, surpassing the Zacks Consensus Estimate by 3.92%. This compares to year-ago revenues of $1.21 billion. The company has topped consensus revenue estimates four times over the last four quarters.

The sustainability of the stock's immediate price movement based on the recently-released numbers and future earnings expectations will mostly depend on management's commentary on the earnings call.

ZIM shares have lost about 5.4% since the beginning of the year versus the S&P 500's decline of -5.3%.
