# Zillow (Z) Lags Q4 Earnings Estimates

February 11, 2025 — 06:05 pm EST

Written by
Zacks Equity Research for
[Zacks->](https://www.nasdaq.com/publishers/zacks)

Zillow (Z) came out with quarterly earnings of $0.27 per share, missing the Zacks Consensus Estimate of $0.29 per share. This compares to earnings of $0.20 per share a year ago. These figures are adjusted for non-recurring items.

This quarterly report represents an earnings surprise of -6.90%. A quarter ago, it was expected that this real estate website operator would post earnings of $0.32 per share when it actually produced earnings of $0.35, delivering a surprise of 9.38%.

Over the last four quarters, the company has surpassed consensus EPS estimates two times.

Zillow, which belongs to the Zacks Real Estate - Operations industry, posted revenues of $554 million for the quarter ended December 2024, surpassing the Zacks Consensus Estimate by 2.41%. This compares to year-ago revenues of $474 million. The company has topped consensus revenue estimates four times over the last four quarters.

The sustainability of the stock's immediate price movement based on the recently-released numbers and future earnings expectations will mostly depend on management's commentary on the earnings call.

Zillow shares have added about 18.8% since the beginning of the year versus the S&P 500's gain of 3.1%.

While the magnitude and direction of estimate revisions could change following the company's just-released earnings report, the current status translates into a Zacks Rank #2 (Buy) for the stock. It will be interesting to see how estimates for the coming quarters and current fiscal year change in the days ahead. The current consensus EPS estimate is $0.50 on $605.44 million in revenues for the coming quarter and $2.15 on $2.58 billion in revenues for the current fiscal year.

Investors should be mindful of the fact that the outlook for the industry can have a material impact on the performance of the stock as well. In terms of the Zacks Industry Rank, Real Estate - Operations is currently in the bottom 18% of the 250 plus Zacks industries. TPG RE Finance Trust (TRTX), another stock in the same industry, has yet to report results for the quarter ended December 2024. The results are expected to be released on February 18.

This commercial real estate finance company is expected to post quarterly earnings of $0.28 per share in its upcoming report, which represents a year-over-year change of +113.7%. The consensus EPS estimate for the quarter has been revised 5.8% lower over the last 30 days to the current level.

TPG RE Finance Trust's revenues are expected to be $39.48 million, up 25.4% from the year-ago quarter.
