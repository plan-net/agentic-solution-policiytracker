# AI in Fashion: Revolutionizing Size and Fit

Fashion e-commerce has witnessed a surge in popularity in recent years, with millions of people shopping for their favorite clothing items online. However, one significant challenge that online fashion platforms face is ensuring size and fit satisfaction for customers. This challenge often leads to a high rate of returns and customer dissatisfaction. In an effort to address this issue, many fashion companies are turning to Artificial Intelligence (AI) solutions to revolutionize the way we approach sizing and fit in the fashion industry.

In this article, we will explore the application of AI in fashion and how it is transforming the way customers experience online shopping. We will delve into the specific challenges faced by AI systems when it comes to size and fit, and discuss the importance of data in developing accurate algorithms. Additionally, we will take a close look at Zalando, one of Europe's leading e-commerce fashion platforms, and examine their mission in addressing the size and fit problem.

## 2️⃣ The Problem Space and Opportunity

Size and fit dissatisfaction is a common issue faced by online shoppers. The process of finding the right size can be complex, resulting in a high rate of returns. In fact, nearly one-third of all online returns stem from size and fit dissatisfaction. This not only poses a challenge for customers but also creates significant business, customer satisfaction, and environmental opportunities.

## 3️⃣ AI-Specific Challenges

The complexities of the size and fit problem in fashion [Present](https://www.toolify.ai/tool/present-app) unique challenges for AI systems. Outdated sizing statistics, variations in size systems across countries and brands, vanity sizing, and the lack of uniform sizing conventions all contribute to the complexity of accurately determining the right size. Moreover, ambiguity in article data, delayed feedback on size and fit, and variations in customer behavior [Patterns](https://www.toolify.ai/tool/patterns) further compound the challenges faced by AI systems in addressing sizing and fit issues.

## 4️⃣ The Importance of Data

Accurate and comprehensive data is crucial for developing effective AI systems in the field of fashion. Zalando relies on various data sources to power its AI algorithms. These sources include data on purchased articles and their selected sizes, return reasons, article visual data, as well as size and fit feedback from in-house fashion experts. Additionally, Zalando leverages data collected through its specialized fashion styling service, Zalon, which incorporates customers' detailed size data and body measurements acquired through body scanning technology.

## 5️⃣ Overview of Zalando

Zalando, one of the leading fashion e-commerce platforms in Europe, attracts millions of customers with its diverse range of brands and over 700,000 articles available for purchase. With around 450 million visits per month and over 38 million active customers, Zalando is at the forefront of the fashion industry. Its multi-disciplinary team, including experts in applied science, aims to tackle the size and fit problem by developing cutting-edge AI systems.

## 6️⃣ The Complications of Size and Fit in Fashion

The complexities of size and fit in fashion extend beyond simple numerical measurements. Garments are described using multiple [Dimensions](https://www.toolify.ai/tool/dimensions), such as waist size, length, and fit. These dimensions cannot be treated uniformly, as the requirements for each garment type vary. Moreover, the concept of vanity sizing adds another layer of complexity, with brands tweaking sizing scales to cater to specific target customers. All these factors contribute to the perplexing challenge of ensuring accurate size and fit recommendations.

## 7️⃣ Challenges in the Problem Space

The Zalando team faces several challenges when it comes to addressing the size and fit problem. Dealing with different size systems, variations across categories, and limited data for newly added articles without sales history poses significant obstacles. Additionally, the high ambiguity level in article data and delayed feedback due to return processes further complicate the task. Understanding the diverse behavior patterns of customers and managing expectations also adds to the challenges faced in this problem space.

## 8️⃣ The Mission of the Zalando Team

The Zalando team has a clear mission: to ensure customers find the right size and fit from the first try. With a multi-disciplinary approach and a diverse set of skills, the team focuses on developing AI systems to address this problem. By leveraging advanced algorithms, incorporating feedback from fashion experts, and utilizing comprehensive data sources, the team aims to enhance the sizing and fit experience for customers.

## 9️⃣ Data Sources Utilized

To create accurate size and fit recommendations, Zalando taps into various data sources. These include information on purchased articles, return reasons, article visual data, and feedback from in-house fashion experts. Additionally, Zalando utilizes data from its fashion styling service, Zalon, where customers provide detailed size data and undergo body scanning. The combination of these data sources [forms](https://www.toolify.ai/category/ai-forms-surveys) the foundation for building effective AI systems.

## 🔟 Algorithms in Production

Zalando employs a range of advanced algorithms to provide accurate size and fit advice to customers. Article-centric size advice utilizes statistical models based on binomial likelihood, which estimate whether an article will have size issues. This approach considers return data along with teacher labels generated from the likelihood model. On the other [HAND](https://www.toolify.ai/tool/hand), customer-centric size advice is personalized based on a customer's purchase history, incorporating deep learning recommenders that utilize customer and article features. The use of meta-learning and reference items further enhances the accuracy of size recommendations, even for customers with limited purchase history.

By leveraging the power of AI, Zalando aims to revolutionize the online fashion industry by addressing the long-standing challenges of size and fit. Through a combination of robust algorithms, comprehensive data, and continuous improvement, Zalando strives to provide customers with a seamless and satisfying shopping experience, ensuring the right size and fit from the first try.

**Q: How does Zalando handle variations in size systems across countries and brands?**
A: Zalando recognizes the challenge posed by variations in size systems and adopts a multi-dimensional approach. They leverage comprehensive data sources, such as purchasers' sizing feedback and return reasons, to develop algorithms that can tackle these variations effectively.

**Q: How does Zalando address the issue of delayed feedback on size and fit?**
A: Zalando factors in the delayed feedback issue by refining their algorithms to account for the time lag between customers' purchases and their return decisions. Additionally, they focus on acquiring timely and accurate feedback from fashion experts to bridge the gap caused by delayed customer feedback.

**Q: Is Zalando able to provide accurate size recommendations for customers with no purchase history?**
A: Yes, Zalando has developed algorithms specifically tailored to cater to customers with no or limited purchase history. Through techniques like customer in the loop and the use of questionnaire attributes, Zalando can provide accurate size recommendations even for cold start customers.

**Q: How does Zalando handle customer expectations in terms of size and fit advice?**
A: Zalando understands the high expectations customers have when it comes to size and fit advice. To meet these expectations, they continuously enhance their algorithms, incorporate state-of-the-art deep learning techniques, and provide explanations and personalized recommendations to customers, ensuring a high level of customer satisfaction.

**Q: How does Zalando acquire size and fit feedback from fashion experts?**
A: Zalando has in-house fashion experts who try on garments and shoes and provide detailed feedback on size and fit. By collecting feedback from these experts, Zalando obtains valuable information on garment attributes, fit, and shape, which further enhances the accuracy of their algorithms.
