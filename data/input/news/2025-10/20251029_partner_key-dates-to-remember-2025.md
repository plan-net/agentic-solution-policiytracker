## **What’s the Assortment relevance review?**

The Assortment relevance process evaluates your articles with regards to their relevance, by using signals we obtain from observed customer behaviour.

When these signals show that an article is not sufficiently relevant to our customers compared to similar articles offered in the same market. The visibility of the article will be reduced locally in the market where their performance was not sufficient or the article will be deactivated globally across all markets to streamline our customers' shopping experience.

## **What’s retagging?**

Retagging is the process of updating an article’s designated season (e.g. a SS24 article can be retagged to SS25). It offers partners the opportunity to keep the products in their collection to be marketed and sold for an additional season at full price.

Please find more information about our season retagging process for Partner Program partners [here.](https://partner.zalando.com/university/article/season-retagging)

And for Connected Retail partners please find more information [here](https://partner.zalando.com/university/article/seasonality-on-zalando).

## **What’s the Basics review?**

Basics articles undergo a monthly review process where they must meet specific criteria. This review ensures that these articles have consistent stock replenishment to meet ongoing customer needs. If an article doesn't meet the requirements, it is converted into a seasonal article.

### **The Basics review requirements**

To remain classified as a basic article, an article must have a:

- Size Availability Rate (SAR) of over 85%

- Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) of over €1000

- And it must fulfill the current image requirements.

The review process will check if the article meets either the SAR or GMV threshold, as well as our [image requirements](https://partner.zalando.com/university/article/zalando-image-guidelines). If these requirements are not met, the article will be converted to a seasonal article.
