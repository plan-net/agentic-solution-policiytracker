# European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS)

[European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS)](https://sosesg.com/en/argomento/european-sustainability-reporting-standards-esrs)

The European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS) specify the sustainability information that an enterprise must disclose in accordance with Directive 2013/34/EU, as amended by Directive 2022/2464/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council.

The information that an enterprise must disclose relates to " _material impacts, risks and opportunities in relation to environmental, social, and governance sustainability matters_".

The term " **material**" indicates that the ESRS do not require enterprises to disclose information on environmental, social, and governance aspects if they have determined that these aspects are not material.

In other words, the disclosed information should enable users of the sustainability report to understand the significant impacts of the enterprise on people and the environment, and the relevant effects of sustainability issues on the enterprise's development, performance, and position ( [ESRS 1, par.2](https://sosesg.com/en/principio-esrs/esrs-1-objective-par-2)).
