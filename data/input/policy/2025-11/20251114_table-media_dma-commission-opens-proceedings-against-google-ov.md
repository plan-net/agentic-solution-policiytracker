# DMA: Commission opens proceedings against Google over anti-spam policy

The company criticizes the Commission's investigation for endangering the quality of searches in Europe. (IMAGO / Zoonar)

In new proceedings, the Commission is targeting Google's “site reputation abuse policy.” According to Google, this is intended to prevent users from seeing misleading, low-quality content and being exposed to fraud attempts.
