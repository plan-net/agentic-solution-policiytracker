France has asked the European Commission to take action against e-commerce group Shein, after the French government said it would suspend the platform over the presence of child-like sex dolls and weapons. 
 The French government on Wednesday initiated suspension procedures against China-founded, Singapore-based Shein after finding sex dolls with child-like characteristics as well as banned weapons such as machetes and brass knuckles for sale by third-party sellers. 
 Ministers called for the EU to exercise powers under the Digital Services Act, under which it can suspend platforms or levy fines of up to 6 percent of a company’s global annual revenue. 
 The BHV department store in Paris, where Shein opened its first-ever physical shop this week. Image credit: Unsplash 
 EU action 
 “France calls on the European Commission to fully exercise these powers — including through the issuance of provisional measures against the platform — and to promptly conduct investigations to shed full light on the malfunctions that led to the marketing of illegal products on this platform,” said French finance minister Roland Lescure and digital minister Anne le Henanff in a letter addressed to technology commissioner Henna Virkkunen. 
 They said France expects “there are similar risks associated with this platform’s activities in other European Union countries”. 
 The Commission said it had received the request and was in touch with Shein over the complaint, saying that violations of EU law would drive next steps, but adding that the EU did not intend to suspend any platforms across the bloc. 
 Shein and China-based competitor Temu have been targeted by regulators around the world for allowing cheap and little-regulated products to flow in large quantities across borders. 
 Toxic metals 
 Germany’s state-backed product testing group Stiftung Warentest said last week that 110 out of 162 items it tested from Shein and Temu did not meet EU standards, listing unsafe toys and jewellery that contained toxic metals. 
 Following complaints over the weekend about the child-like sex dolls, Shein barred sales of all sex dolls worldwide. 
 Following a French complaint on Wednesday, it suspended all third-party sales in France, saying this would “strengthen accountability and ensure every product meets our standards and legal obligations”.
