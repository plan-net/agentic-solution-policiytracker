# EU probes Google over alleged news site suppression

The European Commission has launched a probe into Google for allegedly demoting some news sites in search results, potentially breaching the Digital Markets Act.

It announced on Thursday indications that the US company is deprioritizing news media and other publishers' websites when they include commercial contents, based on its policy.

The Commission said the policy appears to directly impact publishers' profits. Google could face fines of up to 10 percent of its worldwide annual turnover if found noncompliant.

Google said the policy exists "to protect people from deceptive, low-quality content and scams." It added that the investigation is "misguided and risks harming millions of European users."
