# EU digital omnibus plan ‘rolls back fundamental rights,’ civil society groups say

( November 13, 2025, 08:00 GMT \| Official Statement) -- MLex Summary: The European Commission’s Digital Omnibus package would dismantle Europe’s protections against digital threats, 127 civil society groups have warned. In a joint letter coordinated by European Digital Rights, or EDRi, they said the plan — presented as a technical simplification — is a deregulation effort that risks reopening the General Data Protection Regulation, ePrivacy rules and AI Act, weakening democratic oversight and rolling back core digital rights protections.The letter is attached....
