# EU Data Act’s draft law to set up national enforcement approved in Germany

( October 29, 2025, 11:00 GMT \| Official Statement) -- MLex Summary: The EU Data Act's implementation is moving ahead in Germany as the federal cabinet approved a draft law establishing the Federal Network Agency as the country’s main enforcement authority. While the EU regulation has already been in force since September, the German bill still requires parliamentary approval before national oversight and complaint mechanisms can begin operating. The proposal aims to simplify data access, minimize bureaucracy and support new digital business models.The press release follows in German....
