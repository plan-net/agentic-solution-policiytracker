# Google Withdraws EU Antitrust Complaint Against Microsoft After New Probe launched

By Edith Hancock 
 
 
Alphabet's Google withdrew a European Union antitrust complaint against Microsoft over its cloud business Azure after EU competition officials opened a probe into the company under new tech rules.

Alphabet's Google withdrew a European Union antitrust complaint against Microsoft over its cloud business Azure after EU competition officials opened a probe into the company under new tech rules. 
 
Google said the withdrawal comes after the European Commission opened investigations into whether Microsoft's Azure and Amazon's Amazon Web Services need to adhere to the Digital Markets Act, legislation governing the digital economy in Europe. 
 
"We filed our antitrust complaint with the European Commission to give voice to our customers and partners about the issue of anticompetitive cloud licensing practices," Giorgia Abeltino, senior director of government affairs at Google Cloud Europe, said on Friday. She said the company stands behind its original arguments. 
 
Other parts of Microsoft and Amazon's businesses are already subject to the DMA, such as Microsoft's Windows PC operating system and Amazon's e-commerce platform. Companies' search engines and app stores typically need to cross certain thresholds for turnover and active users to come under the scope of the rules, but user numbers are more difficult to determine for large cloud providers that offer their software through commercial contracts. 
 
Microsoft declined to comment. The commission did not immediately respond to a request for comment. 
 
The commission said earlier this month that it can still use the DMA to check whether companies act as important gateways between businesses and consumers for services that don't meet usual thresholds. 
 
Google initially filed an antitrust complaint against Microsoft in 2024, saying the company used unfair software licensing practices that made it harder for rivals to compete. That complaint came shortly after Microsoft settled an earlier dispute with cloud industry association CISPE.
