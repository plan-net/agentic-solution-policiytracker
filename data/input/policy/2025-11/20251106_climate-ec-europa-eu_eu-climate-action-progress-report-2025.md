# EU Climate Action Progress Report 2025

EU Climate Action Progress Report 2025

Strengthening competitiveness on the road to climate neutrality

**Why do we publish this report?** The European Commission monitors and assesses progress in meeting climate targets, as required by EU legislation. Under Articles 29(1) and 29(5) of the [Governance Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02018R1999-20231120), it must assess and report on progress annually by the end of October, starting from 2021. This Climate Action Progress Report serves as this assessment.
