# European Commission deprioritises sustainability reporting standards for non-EU companies

By [Sam Groves](https://www.corporatedisclosures.org/authors/sam-groves.html)

The European Commission’s Directorate-General for Financial Stability, Financial Services and Capital Markets Union (DG FISMA) has issued a list of Level 2 regulations that have been deemed “non-essential” and will therefore be deprioritised.

This list includes the Delegated Acts on sector-specific ESRS, sustainability reporting standards for listed SMEs (LSME) and sustainability reporting standards for companies based outside of the EU.

The Delegated Act on the standard for non-EU companies was due to be adopted in 2026. However, according to the DG FISMA list, it will not be adopted before October 2027.

[Full list of non-essential regulations (DG FISMA)](https://finance.ec.europa.eu/document/download/5240f9bf-d996-45bc-8b33-04981a2a0ef0_en?filename=251001-level-2-esas-simplification-annex_en.pdf)
