# Digital Services Act and amendments to Poland’s Electronic Services Act

# Digital Services Act and amendments to Poland’s Electronic Services Act

[Answer in writing](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/E-10-2025-000131-ASW_EN.html)

Question for written answer  E-000131/2025

Kosma Złotowski (ECR)

Poland needs to bring its legislation in line with the Digital Services Act (DSA). As part of the process, it is amending its Electronic Services Act.

The draft revision of this act prepared by the Ministry of Digital Affairs, as the ministry responsible, envisages empowering the President of the Office of Electronic Communications to issue orders expressly blocking online content in the context of administrative proceedings. Concerns have been raised, including by experts, about the possible misuse of this measure to restrict freedom of speech online and censure (albeit lawful) content and material criticising, for example, government policy, politicians or other public figures.

- 1.Does the Commission believe that such a legal solution could be deemed a threat to the legal guarantee of freedom of speech, particularly as the President of the Office of Electronic Communications is appointed and dismissed by the Sejm on the motion of the Prime Minister, laying their decisions open to political pressure.
- 2.In the Commission’s view, is the mechanism put forward by the Ministry of Digital Affairs for removing online content compatible with the objectives and scope of the DSA, and also with the guarantee of the freedom of expression, particularly as any appeal against a decision of the President of the Office of Electronic Communications will be subject to only a secondary limited judicial review, and the procedure determining the illegality of content will take place without the involvement of the author of the content at issue?
