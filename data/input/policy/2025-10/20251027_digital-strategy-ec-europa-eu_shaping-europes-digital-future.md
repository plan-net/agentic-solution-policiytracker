# Shaping Europe’s digital future

## The Apply AI Strategy sets out how to speed up the use of AI in Europe’s key industries and the public sector

AI is transforming how businesses operate, reshaping public services, and revolutionising science. With these strategies, the Commission is delivering on its AI Continent Action Plan, setting a course to make Europe a global leader in trustworthy AI.
