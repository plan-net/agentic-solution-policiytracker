# Klarna Sues Google for $8.3 Billion Over Shopping Comparison Practices

By [Ainvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/author/)

Monday, Oct 20, 2025 11:07 pm ET1min read

A Swedish court has begun hearing a lawsuit filed by Klarna against Google for $8.3 billion, alleging that Google breached EU antitrust laws by promoting its own shopping comparisons in search results. The case stems from a European Union General Court ruling in 2022 and a European Commission decision in 2017. Klarna is seeking damages based on economic analysis of losses incurred. Google has opposed the lawsuit and claims that changes it made in response to the European Commission's decision have been effective.
