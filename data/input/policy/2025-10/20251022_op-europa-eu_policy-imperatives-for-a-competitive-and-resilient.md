Publication metadata 
 
 Drawing on insights from 44 EU-funded R&amp;I projects, this publication outlines the case for a Nature-Positive Economy (NPE) and the pathways to achieve it. It shows how embedding nature at the core of EU policies can secure Europe’s resilience, prosperity, and global leadership. Urgent action is needed through strategic investment, coherent regulation, empowered local engagement, and systemic reform. Transitioning to a NPE is essential to deliver the European Green Deal, the EU Biodiversity Strategy, and the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework, ensuring economic growth within planetary boundaries while restoring nature for future generations.
