# Commission and EDPB launch consultation on EU DMA-GDPR joint guidelines

[Copyright © 2025 LexisNexis](https://www.lexisnexis.com/terms/copyright.aspx)

Published on:09 October 2025

Published by [LNB News](https://www.lexisnexis.co.uk/legal/news)

Issue Date: 09 October 2025

Published Date: 09 October 2025

Jurisdiction(s): European Union

The European Commission and the European Data Protection Board (EDPB) have launched a consultation on draft Joint Guidelines addressing the interplay between the EU Digital Markets Act (EU DMA) and Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)). The Joint Guidelines aim to ensure coherent and effective application of the two regulatory frameworks, which have complementary objectives and several points of intersection, while respecting the distinct competences of both the Commission and the EDPB. The guidelines provide guidance to gatekeepers on how to interpret and comply with both sets of rules. The consultation seeks feedback particularly from business users, including small and medium enterprises, and end users of gatekeepers' digital services within the scope of the DMA, as well as associations representing these users. The Commission and the EDPB will review all submissions before adopting the final Joint Guidelines in 2026. The consultation closes on 4 December 2025.
