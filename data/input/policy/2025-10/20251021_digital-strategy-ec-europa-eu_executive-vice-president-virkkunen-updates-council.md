# Executive Vice-President Virkkunen updates Council and Parliament on simplification, implementation and enforcement of the digital rulebook

Commission Executive Vice-President for Tech Sovereignty, Security and Democracy Henna Virkkunen has recently transmitted her first Annual Progress Report to Council and Parliament.

This Commission’s agenda of implementation and simplification was kicked off by the [political guidelines](https://commission.europa.eu/document/e6cd4328-673c-4e7a-8683-f63ffb2cf648_en) adopted at the start of the mandate.

In her report, which covers the period from 1 January to 31 July 2025, the Executive Vice-President notably outlines the [upcoming Digital Simplification Package](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-collects-feedback-simplify-rules-data-cybersecurity-and-artificial-intelligence-upcoming). The report also goes into the Commission’s efforts to implement and enforce EU digital rules such as the Digital Services Act and the Artificial Intelligence Act.

Along with the [implementation dialogues](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-making-process/better-regulation/simplification-and-implementation/implementation-dialogues_en), the Annual Progress Reports are embedded in the new working methods of this mandate of the Commission.

Event report \|
