## Proceedings initiated against Temu on suspicion of influencing marketplace sellers’ pricing\*

Bonn, 8 October 2025: The Bundeskartellamt has today initiated proceedings against Whaleco Technology Limited (Temu), based in Dublin, Ireland. The purpose of the proceedings is to examine the terms and conditions for sellers used by Temu on its German online marketplace under the domain temu.com, and other practices used by Temu when dealing with these sellers.
