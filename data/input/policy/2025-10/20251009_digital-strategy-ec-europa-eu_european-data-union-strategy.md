# European Data Union Strategy

The Data Union Strategy will increase the availability of data for AI development, announce the simplification of EU data rules and strengthen the EU’s global position on international data flows.

Building upon the [2020 European strategy for data](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/strategy-data), the **Data Union Strategy** is coming soon. The strategy intends to **scale up the use and availability of data for AI** in Europe through targeted measures that guarantee European businesses gain access to high-quality, large-scale, real-world data.

Among other things, the strategy will promote the use of [data spaces](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/data-spaces) as decentralised, trust-based systems to facilitate secure data sharing among organisations while allowing them to maintain control over their data. Moreover, the strategy includes investment plans in data technologies and infrastructures.

The data Union will also lay out our vision how to create a **more coherent and simplified legal framework for data sharing** by drawing on existing rules, such as the [Data Act](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/data-act) and the [Data Governance Act](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/data-governance-act). Our companies need to be able to spend more time innovating and less time on paperwork.

The strategy aims to **strengthen the EU’s global position** through a strategic and assertive approach to international data flows. As geopolitical tensions and digital protectionism grow, Europe must safeguard its assets and shape global rules so data can flow to and from Europe securely and in a trusted manner.
